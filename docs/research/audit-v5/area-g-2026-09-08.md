## Cross-cutting finding AV5-003 / #627

Area E already demonstrated a real initialization/reconstruction integration defect:

- explicit-split spatial execution initializes synthetic founders using the authoritative `populationSeed`;
- M9 history replay reconstructs synthetic founders from process `ExperimentConfig.seed`;
- when those seed roles differ, an otherwise valid spatial M9 run can reject itself because replay begins from the wrong founder residences.

This is P2 because the demonstrated path fails closed rather than silently validating an incorrect history. It is nevertheless directly relevant to Area G because the authoritative initial population realization cannot currently be reconstructed correctly by that continuation/integrity seam.

AV5-003 remains open and intentionally unrepaired until A–N discovery completes.

## Burn-in/path-dependence interpretation review

The frozen contracts continue to state appropriately that:

- synthetic founder state is an engineering/null-model initialization, not a neutral prehistoric prior;
- declared founder geography, age structure, condition, genealogy and reproductive history are causal initial conditions;
- initial resource stock is an explicit initial-condition assumption distinct from storage capacity;
- elapsed time alone is not evidence of burn-in or equilibrium;
- equilibrium-like claims require trajectory diagnostics plus run-length and analysis-window sensitivity;
- multiple stable regimes or persistent initialization effects must be retained rather than hidden by pooling.

No fresh contradiction between these interpretation rules and the tested executable behavior was found.
