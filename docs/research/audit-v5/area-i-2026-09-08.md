The fresh adversary held the one-person, one-cell resource-pressure scenario fixed and varied only `ResourceConfig.periodsPerYear` across `1, 2, 4, 12, 52, 365`. Mortality, fertility, migration, condition recovery/loss and scarcity mortality were disabled so the measured effect was the finite-capacity resource update clock itself.

Exact curve:

```text
P=1:   regenerated=0,   harvested=350, unmet=650, gap_to_daily=350 (500 permille)
P=2:   regenerated=176, harvested=526, unmet=474, gap_to_daily=174 (248 permille)

```

For every cadence:

- annual need was exactly 1,000;
- `harvested + unmet = annual need` exactly;
- `initial stock + regenerated = harvested + final stock` exactly;
- final stock was zero;
- regeneration and harvest were monotone non-decreasing under refinement;
- unmet need was monotone non-increasing under refinement.

The coarse-clock effect from #623 is therefore quantitatively material but behaved as a smooth declared resolution dependence in this stress case. Weekly (`P=52`) was 0.8% below the daily endpoint, monthly-ish (`P=12`) was 4.0% below it, and quarterly (`P=4`) was 12.4% below it.

**Disposition: no new finding.** Resource-pressure conclusions must remain conditioned on the declared M3 clock and practical-clock robustness, but this controlled refinement did not demonstrate an implementation discontinuity, accounting failure or hidden clock choice.

## Spatial-resolution and numerical-convergence semantics

Frozen v0.3.5 explicitly identifies current spatial behaviour as `cell_space_resolution_dependent_v1`; it does not claim raster-refinement invariance. `SpatialScaleAssessment` preserves cell size and the model-facing bases for M2 reproductive locality, M3 per-cell resources, M4 grid-step distance and M9 grid-edge travel cost, and marks `requiresResolutionSensitivity = true`.

The normative research-use rule already requires a study making physical spatial inference to either:

1. run a predeclared plausible raster-resolution sensitivity/convergence design and establish adequate stability;
2. report material resolution dependence and condition the claim on the selected raster; or
3. use a later separately identified physically normalized model.

**Disposition: no new finding.** The present spatial model is resolution-dependent by declared scientific semantics rather than silently presented as a converged physical discretization. This remains a substantive study-level limitation, not evidence that any particular raster is empirically adequate.

## Cross-cutting robustness limitations retained

Area I is not scientifically clean even though these two fresh Area-I adversaries produced no new issue.

- **AV5-001/#606 (P1):** global coupling-rank renumbering can alter an unchanged focal household's M9 equal-cost destination after adding an isolated founder. That is a genuine robustness/locality failure and remains relevant to sensitivity interpretation.
- **#612:** same process seeds across structural arms are replicate blocking/coupling, not a guarantee of per-agent common-random-number invariance. Structural comparisons must not interpret same-seed differences as perfectly draw-matched individual counterfactuals.
- **AV5-005/#640 (P1):** the small-n normal-CLT `mean` precision gate can stop with severely under-covered nominal intervals. Replicate-count or Monte Carlo convergence claims using that affected estimator cannot be treated as established merely because the current gate reports sufficient precision.
- **Spatial scale:** current cell-space semantics are intentionally resolution-dependent, so robustness across raster resolution is a study obligation rather than an assumed numerical property.

Fresh Area-G evidence also keeps initialization/path-dependence checks visible, including exact future-horizon common-prefix behaviour and declared-founder synthetic-knob controls; those do not erase the independent Area-I obligation to test alternative initializations in substantive studies.
