The scientific oracle is half-open in time: a newborn produced during an active visit must contribute visitor exposure only after its birth day, not before it. Visitor-household-days must remain unchanged, and all temporary-observability levels must reconcile.

Disposition: **no finding**. Aggregation observability updates living visitor headcount correctly when demography changes household size during an active visit.
