## Scope

This record prepares the already-reviewed post-Audit-v5 repaired state for a named v0.3.6 patch release. Audit v5 is not reopened. No new simulator mechanism or causal-semantics change is introduced by the package/version synchronization.

Audit v5 discovery and remediation are complete: 14/14 Areas A–N were challenged from zero against immutable v0.3.5/v33; 8 findings were demonstrated (4 P1, 4 P2); all 8 are closed; and all four P1 repairs received independent post-merge adversarial re-verification. The final production repair advanced living model semantics to v35.

## Convergence decision

Audit v5 did **not** achieve the project's P1-clean convergence criterion because it discovered four new P1 findings. Subsequent repair/re-verification closes those defects but does not retroactively make the audit a clean convergence pass.

Therefore v0.3.6 is intentionally a **repaired convergence-audit baseline**, not authorization to begin the first empirical/site-specific AnthroSim study. Under the standing audit rule, another fresh full A–N audit is required against the newly frozen v0.3.6/v35 release. If that next audit is non-clean, the repair/freeze/audit cycle repeats. A site-specific first question should wait for a future P1-clean convergence pass.

## Identity decision

v0.3.6 freezes **model semantics v35**. Software/package release identity advances from 0.3.5 to 0.3.6, while `MODEL_SEMANTICS_ID` remains v35 because release naming itself does not alter authoritative scientific behaviour.

The immutable v0.3.5/v33 tag remains the Scientific Audit-v5 discovery target. Audit-v5 discovery/evidence documents therefore retain v0.3.5/v33 where that is their historical identity. Rewriting those records to v0.3.6/v35 would destroy provenance rather than improve consistency.
