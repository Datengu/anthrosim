## Positive findings

### Complete typed research configuration is preserved

The #205 research path no longer reconstructs a narrow subset from hidden synthetic defaults. `ResearchRunConfig` embeds the complete authoritative `ExperimentConfig` and optional exact spatial configuration. Dimension values are applied through typed serialization/deserialization and the resolved configuration is then validated through the ordinary simulation constructor.

Unknown paths, wrong JSON types, invalid integer widths, reserved schema/seed/semantics paths and invalid resolved configurations fail closed. Seed variation is kept separate from scientific coordinates.

This substantially resolves the historical hidden-configuration problem that motivated #205.

### Numerical/spatial dependence is not falsely labelled convergence

The #203 contract explicitly declares present evidence-grounded spatial execution `cell_space_resolution_dependent_v1`. Its metamorphic fixtures preserve several known non-convergences rather than hiding them: the same 200 m × 200 m surface represented at 50 m rather than 100 m resolution has four times as many per-cell resource opportunities when cell productivity is held fixed; a 3-cell M4 horizon changes from 300 m to 150 m; and a uniform 200 m M9 route changes from 2 to 4 grid edges, doubling abstract route cost from 2,000 to 4,000 in the documented fixture. A study must therefore either demonstrate resolution robustness or condition the claim on raster scale.

### Long-run convergence is claim-conditional and adversarial

The #220 diagnostic does not equate long runtime with equilibrium. It distinguishes stable, cyclic-stable, drifting and insufficient-data trajectories; requires duration-reached runs for an equilibrium-like gate; tests predeclared run-length and alternative analysis-window sensitivity; and reports multiple stable regimes rather than pooling them into one mean.

This is an appropriate robustness contract for a simulator with persistent genealogy, resources, movement and initialization memory.

### Structural sensitivity has produced material effects

The checked-in first household-lifecycle comparison demonstrates that structural assumptions can materially affect outputs. Across eight paired 40-year seeds, size fission versus fixed founder households changed total unmet resource need from 962 to 449 (-53.3%), M4 moves from 85 to 97 (+14.1%), M9 departures from 7,336 to 8,046 (+9.7%), and visitor household-days from 51,317 to 56,280 (+9.7%), while visitor person-days changed only -0.2%.

Those numbers are evidence that structural robustness must be evaluated rather than assumed. However, AV2-004/#324 means the current fission membership rule contains PersonId/birth-order social composition and should not be treated as a defensible neutral alternative until repaired/rechecked.

## Adversarial finding AV2-010 / #336 — metadata can masquerade as structural treatment

The v1 research definition correctly says that free-form labels such as `modelId` or `scheduleId` do not by themselves establish executable structural variation. The machine validator does not enforce that rule.

`ResearchDimension::validate_against(...)` accepts any existing non-numeric target/value as `kind: structural` except a small set of reserved schema/semantics paths. Normal M2 validation only requires `demography.scheduleId` to be non-empty; the string is not consumed by transition dynamics.

Therefore a definition may legally declare:

```json
{
  "id": "demography_structure",
  "kind": "structural",
  "path": "/experiment/demography/scheduleId",
  "values": ["alternative_a", "alternative_b"]
}
```

The design expands to two different point identities carrying a structural coordinate, but the executable demographic schedule is identical. The same is true for provenance-only enum fields and analogous model labels.

### Quantitative consequence

For `k` distinct metadata labels on such a path:

- nominal structural alternatives: `k`;
- distinct executable model structures: **1**;
- same-seed causal contrast: exactly **0** by construction.

Thus 2, 5 or 20 nominal structural arms can all agree perfectly without testing any structural uncertainty. A downstream study that trusts the preserved `kind: structural` classification can then overstate robustness across model formulations.

Issue #336 records the required repair: distinguish causal executable fields from metadata/provenance coordinates, reject metadata-only paths as structural treatments (or classify them explicitly as non-treatment metadata), while retaining whole-object alternatives that really alter executable structure.

Severity is P2: ordinary trajectories and valid numeric sensitivity are unaffected, but the research-design layer can certify a misleading structural-sensitivity design.

## Dependency dispositions

### #326 / AV2-005 — temporal convergence remains invalid under partial supply

Area D already demonstrated that holding annual maximum condition-loss budget and supply fraction fixed does not preserve annual deterioration as M3 resolution changes: at a constant 1-permille deficit, annual loss ranges from 1 point at one settlement/year to 365 points at 365 settlements/year. Any resource temporal-resolution convergence result involving partial supply is therefore blocked until #326 is repaired and independently reverified.

### #334 / AV2-009 — tail/quantile Monte Carlo convergence remains invalid

Area H demonstrated that the current nominal 95% quantile interval can have only 33.08% exact coverage for the 95th percentile at n=8, and similarly severe under-coverage in other supported cases. A passing current quantile precision gate cannot be used as evidence that tail sensitivity estimates have converged until #334 is repaired and independently reverified.

### #324 / AV2-004 — household structural sensitivity must be repeated after repair

The material fission-vs-fixed effects above show genuine sensitivity to the implemented treatment, but the treatment itself sorts social composition by PersonId/birth-append order. The relevant structural comparison must therefore be rerun after #324 is corrected before its effect magnitude/direction is used as evidence about a neutral household-lifecycle uncertainty.

## Area I disposition

Area I is **complete — findings**.

The central sensitivity infrastructure is substantially stronger than the historical M7-only surface: complete typed configuration, explicit structural coordinates, provenance-bound Cartesian designs, long-run diagnostics, declared spatial non-convergence and support-scale reporting all exist. The audit found one new research-design validity defect (#336) and carried forward three material dependencies (#324, #326, #334) that prevent particular robustness claims from being treated as settled.

No claim is made that AnthroSim has completed a study-specific global sensitivity analysis for a real archaeological application. The infrastructure enables such analyses; defensible parameter ranges, sampling design, interaction analysis and empirical interpretation remain study-specific work.

Area J should distinguish sensitivity from identifiability: a parameter can strongly affect outputs yet remain non-identifiable because another parameter or structural hypothesis compensates for it. Existing issue #217 is directly relevant and should be checked against current executable/analysis support before creating any new finding.

Preserve #326 and #334 as P1 repair/reverification dependencies and #336 as the new Area I P2 finding.
