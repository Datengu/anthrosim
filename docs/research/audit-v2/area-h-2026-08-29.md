## Positive evidence

### RNG and paired-seed semantics

`crates/anthrosim-core/src/rng.rs` derives stable named ChaCha8 streams from the master seed and stores/restores exact stream positions. The core regression deliberately demonstrates the state-dependent-consumption property identified by historical #214: paired seeds share stream definitions but do not preserve agent/event-level random shocks after treatment-induced divergence.

It treats pairing as replicate blocking/coupling, requires the uncertainty estimator to match the design, and forbids interpreting one seed pair as a literal same-agent counterfactual history. This is a scientifically appropriate disposition of #214; no new RNG-stream or paired-seed interpretation defect was demonstrated.

### Extinction / survivor conditioning

`docs/research/survivor-conditioned-estimands-v1.md` explicitly treats mean living condition as conditional on survival, keeps it undefined after extinction, and requires joint survival/population reporting for between-arm interpretation. This addresses a major post-treatment-selection failure mode rather than silently averaging extinct and surviving trajectories as though they represented the same estimand.

### Monte Carlo precision architecture

The #231 gate has several strong controls: exact seed identities, frozen fixed/sequential seed batches, no partial-batch peeking, a precision rather than sign/significance stopping rule, study-protocol binding, and distinct methods for means, independent differences, paired differences, Bernoulli probabilities and quantiles. Reproducibility is explicitly separated from replicate sufficiency.

## Adversarial quantile-coverage test

The supported quantile implementation in `scripts/research-monte-carlo-sufficiency.py` computes the nominal rank interval from

```text
rank_center = p * (n - 1)
rank_half   = z * sqrt(n * p * (1-p))
lower_index = floor(rank_center - rank_half)
upper_index = ceil(rank_center + rank_half)
```

clipped to the observed sample range. It then decides sufficiency solely from the resulting value-space half-width.

For a continuous distribution, if `K ~ Binomial(n,p)` is the count below the true population p-quantile, exact coverage of emitted order-statistic interval `[X_(l+1), X_(u+1)]` is

```text
P(l + 1 <= K <= u).
```

The independent audit checker `area-h-quantile-coverage-audit.py` reproduces current rank selection and computes exact binomial coverage. For nominal 95% intervals:

| p | n | current 0-based ranks | exact coverage |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.50 | 2 | [0,1] | 50.00% |
| 0.90 | 8 | [4,7] | 56.45% |
| 0.95 | 8 | [5,7] | **33.08%** |
| 0.95 | 20 | [16,19] | **62.56%** |
| 0.99 | 20 | [17,19] | **18.11%** |
| 0.99 | 100 | [96,99] | **61.56%** |

The 95th-percentile / eight-replicate case is especially clear: a result labelled as a 95% interval has only about 33.1% distribution-free finite-sample coverage. The problem is not specific to one assumed continuous distribution; this binomial-rank calculation is the standard distribution-free coverage argument for order-statistic quantile intervals.

Because current sufficiency depends on observed value-space width, a tightly clustered sample can still produce `sufficient_stop` despite rank support being mathematically incapable of the requested confidence coverage.

## Finding

**AV2-009 / #334 / P1 — Monte Carlo quantile gate can certify severely under-covered intervals as sufficient.**

This is a material scientific-conclusion risk under the protocol's P1 definition: a supported confirmatory tail/quantile analysis can be declared spuriously precise. Quantile/tail stability was explicitly part of #231's purpose, so a warning in prose that small/tail samples may need a stronger method is insufficient when the executable gate still accepts and labels those cases at the requested `confidenceLevel`.

The repair should use a finite-sample-valid rank construction (for example exact binomial/order-statistic bounds) or fail closed when the requested confidence, quantile and sample size cannot support the declared coverage. Narrow observed values must not substitute for sufficient rank information.
