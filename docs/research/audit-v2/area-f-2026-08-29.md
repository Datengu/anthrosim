## Mechanism audit

### Temporary concentration is distinct from relocation

M9 preserves persistent residence separately from temporary presence. Visitor days are attributed to a destination without changing permanent residence or permanent occupied-cell statistics. M4 permanent migration remains a separate transition and is disabled in the frozen M9.7 capability benchmark. This is the required semantic distinction between intermittent aggregation and relocation.

The observability layer exposes resident person-days, visitor person-days, visitor household-days, peak and mean visitor presence, days with any visitors, journey starts/arrivals/returns/completions, origin catchments and travel burden. It also preserves journey-level timing/household identity. Therefore downstream analysis need not infer aggregation solely from a single total-use statistic.

### Duration/exposure and resource consequences

M9's resource ledger attributes at-residence and transit provisioning to the persistent home cell and visiting duration to the temporary destination. Each household's period-duration categories must exactly conserve the elapsed period. Integer need is partitioned without creating or destroying household demand, and supply from multiple cells is reconciled back to one household satisfaction fraction.

The public resource tests deliberately use a one-person, one-period fixture with annual need 365 units. A one-day visit shifts destination stock by exactly one unit; a five-day visit shifts it by exactly five units. This directly guards against the historical error where visits wholly between resource boundaries disappeared from M3 accounting.

M4's local resource-support cue separately uses the current M9 presence state at its decision boundary. Visiting persons contribute to destination provisioning demand, while transit remains home-provisioned. Temporary crowding can therefore affect permanent-migration utility without being reclassified as permanent residence. Historical #196 is the repaired antecedent for this cross-mechanism consistency.

### Boundary/tie/order semantics

The active duration contract specifies resource-boundary order as: accrue elapsed presence, settle M3, reset the duration ledger, apply M9 transitions, then evaluate M4. This prevents a transition exactly on a boundary from rewriting the preceding resource interval. Exact 50/50 integer-need rounding is balanced by the v19 deterministic semantic-side-symmetric rule rather than permanently privileging home or visitor demand; historical #194 is the repaired antecedent.

The Area E equal-cost destination issue #327 concerns stale travel-contract documentation, not a newly demonstrated aggregation-accounting bias here. Its executable tie repair remains relevant because destination choice determines where visitor/resource exposure accumulates.

## Independent quantitative/adversarial test

`area-f-aggregation-audit.py` is intentionally independent of AnthroSim implementation code. It reads the preserved M9.7 machine-readable reference and recomputes the principal paired contrasts directly from the raw pair fields.

Frozen design inspected:

- seeds: `9701..9708` (`n=8` paired seeds);
- horizon: 3,650 days / 10 years;
- 2,000 synthetic founders;
- 10x10 synthetic world;
- 70-cell focal region;
- M4 disabled;
- nine 30-day common-arrival visitor windows in treatment, 270 scheduled visitor days total;
- continuous control with no in-window M9 trigger.

Independent recomputation from the checked-in reference produced:

|---|---:|---:|
| Median treatment added focal person-days / control resident person-days | 31.260722 permille | <= 50 permille pairwise |

All eight preserved pairs have exactly equal resident person-days across arms, zero control visitor person-days, exactly 270 treatment days with visitor presence, positive completed treatment journeys, and zero permanent migrations/condition-mediated deaths. The capability classification therefore remains supported by the machine-readable reference.

### Same-output-pattern adversarial construction

Aggregate focal person-days alone are non-identifying. A simple counterexample has exactly 600 visitor-person-days under two structurally different schedules:

- concentrated: 20 visitors for 30 days;
- diffuse: 10 visitors for 60 days.

Both have identical total temporary exposure, yet peak visitor concentration and days-with-visitors differ by factors of two. AnthroSim's M9 observability explicitly preserves both quantities, so the frozen benchmark does not commit the error of declaring the regimes distinguishable from total person-days alone.

More generally, aggregate summaries can still conceal who overlapped with whom. Current M9 preserves journey-level household timing so co-presence can be reconstructed externally, but it does **not** model social interaction opportunity, exchange, gathering purpose, encounter networks or interaction consequences. Those remain explicit future model dimensions rather than hidden mechanisms. No defect is filed merely because an intentionally unimplemented social mechanism is absent.

## Finding AV2-007 / issue #329 — stale current M9.7 result narrative

A direct cross-check found that `docs/research/m9-controlled-aggregation-benchmark-result.md` calls its v10 execution the **current regression reference** and reports:

- workflow `32930245559`;
- maximum paired focal-person-day difference 36 permille;
- median intermittent peak share 426 permille;
- minimum intermittent peak share 387 permille.

But the machine-readable reference checked into the same protected-main commit records model semantics v18, workflow `33182836825`, and values 37, 432 and 405 permille respectively after integer rounding. The exact independent calculations above agree with the v18 JSON, not the v10 narrative.

This does not overturn `capability_distinguished`; it is a P2 TRACE/result-provenance defect because a current-facing scientific result document cites obsolete execution identity and quantitative results. Issue #329 records the demonstrated mismatch. The reference verifier intentionally compares scientific configuration/state and paired output projections rather than build/source-bound experiment IDs, so the presence of a historical reference model-semantics ID is not by itself evidence of an executable failure.

## Disposition

**Area F status: complete — findings.**

No new P0/P1 executable aggregation defect was demonstrated. The audited model correctly separates persistent residence from temporary presence, duration-weights resource exposure, lets temporary crowding affect the declared M4 resource cue, preserves temporal concentration observables, and exposes enough journey-level timing to avoid relying on aggregate person-days as an interaction proxy.

The principal demonstrated defect is AV2-007/#329 (P2 current-result/provenance drift). The absence of modeled social interaction opportunity is recorded as an accepted capability boundary, not empirical validation or a claim that aggregation has no social consequences.
