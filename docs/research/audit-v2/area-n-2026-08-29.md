## 1. Demography × households

**Disposition: finding-dependent; repeat required after #324 repair.**

Area C demonstrated that deterministic size fission can sort membership by PersonId/birth append order, including placing four same-day newborns into an autonomous new household. The same historical structural-sensitivity comparison produced large demographic differences between fixed households and the implemented fission treatment: late growth approximately `-0.002%/yr` versus `-0.994%/yr`, mate limitation `11.3%` versus `38.7%`, and mean year-240 population `108.2` versus `28.9`.

These numbers demonstrate that household structure can dominate demographic outcomes, but #324 means the magnitude/direction of the current fission contrast cannot be generalized to a neutral household-turnover mechanism. No second defect is created here; the integrated demographic conclusion is explicitly conditional on repairing/redefining the household partition rule.

## 2. Demography × resources

**Disposition: blocked by P1 #326; integrated consequence quantitatively demonstrated.**

Area D showed that a constant 1-permille supply deficit with the same annual maximum-loss budget yields annual condition loss of 1 at one M3 settlement/year but 365 at 365 settlements/year.

Starting from condition 1000, the resulting one-year conditions are therefore approximately:

- P=1: `999`;
- P=365: `635`.

Condition feeds condition-mediated mortality, so this resolution artifact changes the mortality exposure path even though annual supply fraction and configured response coefficient are held fixed. Demographic/resource conclusions using partial supply must be repeated after #326 repair.

## 3. Households × movement

## 4. Movement × resources

**Disposition: blocked by P1 #326; threshold crossing demonstrated.**

Default M4 `conditionPressureThresholdPermille` is 900. Under the Area D constant 1-permille deficit counterexample:

- P=1 leaves condition 999, above the 900 pressure threshold;
- P=365 can leave condition 635, below the threshold.

Therefore changing only M3 temporal resolution can switch the M4 condition-pressure state from **not triggered** to **triggered** under the same annual supply fraction. This is a qualitative movement decision difference produced by #326, not merely a small condition discrepancy.

M9 presence also changes home/visitor resource demand, so aggregation/mobility can feed back into M3 scarcity; repeat relevant coupled references after #326 repair.
