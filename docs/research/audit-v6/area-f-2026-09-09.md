## Fresh pass 1 — repeated aggregation trigger at the active/completion boundary

Evidence-only PR: **#716**  
Final controlled head: `71a0b48b8bb38a1afffb05ac50585202661f2dbd`  
Central CI run: `34367396021`  
`Quality and tests` job: `102519609766`

The adversary used one declared 30-year-old founder in one household, one focal destination, disabled mortality/fertility/resource demand/M4 migration, exactly one day of travel each way and a five-day visit.

The first departure trigger was fixed at day 100, giving:

- arrival: day 101;
- return departure: day 106;
- completion: day 107.

Two paired arms differed only in the second trigger day:

- **day 106** — the trigger occurs after the household has entered return transit and must be recorded as explicitly ineligible because an active journey remains;
- **day 107** — the trigger occurs on the exact first-journey completion day, where frozen M9 ordering requires completion before new departures and therefore permits an immediate second journey.

Predeclared exact expectations all passed:

- day-106 arm: 2 trigger outcomes, 1 journey started/completed, 1 `ActiveJourney` skip, departures `[100]`, completions `[107]`, visitor person-days `5`;
- day-107 arm: 2 trigger outcomes, 2 journeys started/completed, no active-journey skip, departures `[100,107]`, completions `[107,114]`, visitor person-days `10`.

The final head passed formatting, Clippy, all **284 pre-existing `anthrosim-core` tests**, the fresh recurrence oracle and the complete workspace suite.

Disposition: **no finding**. Repeated aggregation opportunity accounting is explicit at this one-day boundary, and completion-before-new-departure semantics behave as declared.

PR #716 was closed unmerged after disposition.

## Fresh pass 2 — zero-duration same-day handoff inflates peak visitor presence

Evidence-only PR: **#717**  
Exact controlled head: `416da690fa9e1a7e3ab99644096b300f3cce1d39`  
Central CI run: `34366149670`  
`Quality and tests` job: `102515335302`  
Finding: **AV6-007 / #718 P2**

The adversary constructed two one-person households approaching the same focal destination from different travel distances. The test derived the actual near/far travel durations from the frozen 7×1 world and set the visit duration to the difference between them.

That forced the near household's half-open visit to end on the exact day the far household's half-open visit began:

```text
near = [102,112)
far  = [112,122)
```

The intervals touch at day 112 but never overlap for positive simulated duration. Therefore the maximum physically simultaneous visitor count is exactly 1.

The controlled head passed formatting, Clippy and all **284 pre-existing `anthrosim-core` tests** before only the fresh scientific oracle failed.

Exact result:

```text
handoff day=112; near=[102,112); far=[112,122); visitor_person_days=20; reported_peak=2; cell_peak=2
```

Visitor person-day accounting is correct: each ten-day visit contributes ten person-days, for 20 total. The global and destination `peakVisitors` values are nevertheless 2.

Source review explains the discrepancy. Temporary observability accrues physical presence over half-open intervals, but `add_current_visitors(...)` updates `peak_visitors` immediately on each arrival event. M9 processes arrivals before same-day return departures, so replay transiently counts both households, records a peak of 2, and then removes the departing visitor. That intermediate state has zero duration and contributes no visitor person-days.

Thus `visitorPersonDays` and `peakVisitors` use inconsistent temporal semantics at the handoff boundary.

### Duplicate search and classification

Mandatory open/closed issue and PR searches covered `peakVisitors`, visitor peak, half-open visitor intervals, same-day arrival/return, zero-duration overlap and temporary observability.

AV6-007 is distinct from:

- #121 / PR #139 — M9.7 benchmark definition and use of peak presence;

- #223 — planned versus realized M9 travel burden;

No existing issue recorded the demonstrated zero-duration handoff inflation.

Severity is **P2**. The authoritative journey lifecycle, visit durations and visitor person-days remain correct, but `peakVisitors` is a scientifically important derived aggregation-intensity observable and can overstate simultaneous presence under same-day cohort handoffs.

AV6-007 was preserved as issue **#718** before PR #717 was closed unmerged.

The model continues to delimit M9 appropriately: it represents temporary presence, travel and resource consequences, but does not silently introduce travel purpose, ritual/economic motivation, encounter networks, mating interactions, convoy structure or other unimplemented social mechanisms. Their absence is therefore not itself an Area-F implementation defect.

AV6-007 does not by itself invalidate the preserved M9.7 `capability_distinguished` result. The fresh experiment has not shown that the current benchmark contains an equivalent handoff boundary or that its classification reverses. The finding does require peak-based aggregation analyses to be treated as temporally inconsistent until repaired and the benchmark is rerun under the repaired observable.
