## Fresh evidence 1 — M9 equal-cost locality under impassable domain padding (#710 → AV6-006/#711)

Evidence PR: **#710**, closed unmerged.  
Final controlled head: `cd1dd83abf1a83911c4c11e3d47fc662175d580a`.  
Central CI run: `34358664992`.  
`Quality and tests` job: `102489797483`.

The corrected head passed formatting and Clippy and all **284 pre-existing anthrosim-core unit tests** before the fresh Area-E oracle failed.

Construction:

- baseline 3×1 world with movement costs `[1000,1000,1000]`;
- origin `CellId(2)` and exactly two focal destinations, `CellId(1)` and `CellId(3)`;
- both destinations are exactly one edge away at accumulated travel cost `1000`;
- padded arm preserves those three cells exactly and appends only `CellId(4)` with movement cost `6000`;
- M9 traversability ceiling is `5000`, so the appended cell is explicitly impassable and cannot enter a route;
- focal region, origin, local route costs, route distances, tie seed, household coupling key and trigger index are unchanged.

Positive controls proved the padding cell non-traversable and confirmed identical local accumulated cost, two-way equal-minimum membership and one-edge route distances before the destination oracle.

Observed result at tie seed 0:

```text
baseline=CellId(1)
padded=CellId(3)
```

Thus a causally isolated change in simulation-domain extent changes the authoritative local M9 destination.

Mechanism: the v35 M9 spatial-equivariance repair builds its destination coupling classes inside a reflection-canonical frame derived from the **complete authoritative world**. The extra unreachable cell changes that global frame even though it cannot alter the local travel graph, and the same stochastic coupling therefore selects the opposite local destination.

Mandatory duplicate search distinguished this from:

- #190 — historical permanent lower-CellId equal-cost bias;
- AV4-007/#500 — arbitrary HouseholdId in the M9 tie key;
- AV5-001/#606 — remote founder/global coupling-rank renumbering;
- AV5-004/#629 — reflection non-equivariance of the causal travel problem itself.

No prior issue recorded this domain-padding locality failure.

Disposition: **AV6-006 / #711 — P1, open**.

Severity is P1 because an analyst-controlled crop/buffer change outside the causal route graph can alter temporary physical presence, visitor resource demand, aggregation/co-presence and downstream spatial/resource/M4 trajectories under the same local scientific state and stochastic realization.
