### Controlled construction

Three controls isolated the initialization contract:

1. `FounderGenealogyStatus::Unspecified` with household lifecycle disabled remained admissible;
2. the identical omitted-link founder state marked `CompleteLivingDirectParents`, with dependency-aware fission enabled, ran and produced two households;
3. the same omitted-link founder state marked `Unspecified`, with dependency-aware fission enabled, was expected to fail closed.

The final head passed formatting and Clippy. All **284 pre-existing `anthrosim-core` tests passed** before the fresh fail-closed oracle failed.

Exact failing assertion:

```text
dependency-aware household fission must require founder genealogy completeness before missing parent links can be interpreted as structural absence
```

### Scientific interpretation

`FounderGenealogyStatus::Unspecified` explicitly means omitted founder parent links are epistemically unknown and cannot be interpreted as evidence that no represented living direct parent exists.

`deterministic_dependency_fission_v2` consumes parent relationships when refining relationship roles and assigning dependents. Missing parent links therefore alter authoritative household topology.

However, ordinary and spatial construction require `CompleteLivingDirectParents` only for active M4 kin weighting, not for dependency-aware household lifecycle. The initializer therefore admits an epistemically incomplete relationship state into a mechanism that consumes missing relationships as structural state.

This was preserved as **AV6-008 / #721 P2** before evidence PR #720 was closed unmerged.

## Path-dependence and burn-in interpretation review

The living contracts remain appropriately conservative on initial conditions:

- synthetic founder state is an engineering/null-model initialization, not a neutral prehistoric prior;
- declared founder geography, age structure, condition, genealogy and reproductive history are causal initial conditions;
- initial resource stock is an explicit initial-condition assumption distinct from storage capacity;
- elapsed time alone is not evidence of burn-in or equilibrium;
- equilibrium-like claims require trajectory diagnostics plus run-length and analysis-window sensitivity;
- multiple stable regimes or persistent initialization effects must remain visible rather than being silently pooled away.

Permanent tests also continue to demonstrate that alternative founder layouts may remain different indefinitely when no relaxation mechanism exists. This is the correct interpretation for path-dependent simulation rather than an automatic assumption of convergence.

No new v6 defect was found in those interpretation boundaries beyond AV6-008.
