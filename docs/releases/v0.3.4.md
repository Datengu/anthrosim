## Interpretation limits

v0.3.4 is a stronger **framework verification/convergence** baseline. It does **not** establish that AnthroSim is empirically calibrated for a real archaeological population, validate a specific archaeological site or another site, prove model correctness, or make every future inference TRACE-complete. Question-specific evidence quality, uncertainty, calibration/validation, applicability and genuinely held-out corroboration remain separate requirements.
