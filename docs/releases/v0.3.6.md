# AnthroSim v0.3.6 — post-Scientific-Audit-v5 repaired baseline

Release date: 2026-09-09
Software version: `0.3.6`
Model semantics: `anthrosim-model-semantics-v35`
Previous release: `v0.3.5` / `anthrosim-model-semantics-v33`

## Purpose

v0.3.6 freezes the fully remediated Scientific Audit v5 line as a named patch baseline. It does not add a new roadmap milestone, and the release-preparation change itself does not alter causal simulator behaviour: authoritative model semantics had already advanced to v35 through reviewed Audit-v5 repairs.

Scientific Audit v5 independently challenged immutable `v0.3.5` / model-semantics-v33 across Areas A–N. Discovery demonstrated **8 findings: 4 P1 and 4 P2**. All eight received their required production dispositions and are closed; all P1 repairs received independent post-merge adversarial re-verification.

## Scientific semantics progression since v0.3.5

Audit-v5 AV5-001/#606 advanced model semantics from v33 to v34 by replacing the M9 equal-cost household coupling identity's globally ordinal population dependence with a household-local demographic equivalence key. AV5-004/#629 then advanced v34 to v35 by making equal-cost M9 destination realization equivariant under the supported grid-reflection group while preserving marginal exchangeability. Other Audit-v5 dispositions did not require another causal semantics identity.

The package-version change from 0.3.5 to 0.3.6 is not itself a model-semantics change. Historical evidence continues to record the exact package/model/source identities under which it was generated.

## Audit-v5 convergence status

**v0.3.6 is not a P1-clean convergence result.** Audit v5 discovered four new P1 findings, so the audit is classified as a **non-clean convergence pass** even though all findings were subsequently repaired and reverified. Under the project's convergence rule, another fresh full A–N audit against the newly frozen repaired baseline is required before the first empirical/site-specific study begins.

The intended next scientific-process step is therefore a new audit generation against immutable v0.3.6/v35, not an extension or restart of Audit v5 and not the start of a site-specific research question.
