# AnthroSim v0.3.1

## Release scope

AnthroSim v0.3.1 is the post-v0.3.0 audit and research-readiness hardening release.

It preserves the audited M9 temporary-mobility and controlled-aggregation capability baseline while incorporating the scientific-audit work completed after v0.3.0. The release does not designate a universal prehistoric demographic baseline, does not calibrate AnthroSim to a real archaeological population, and does not add target-population feedback.

## Scientific and reproducibility hardening

Since v0.3.0, the audit has strengthened the framework around reproducible scientific inference, including:

- complete scientific configuration exposure to ensemble and sensitivity experiments;
- explicit stochastic-replicate precision/sufficiency gates for quantitative conclusions;
- long-run regime, drift, path-dependence and equilibrium-claim diagnostics;
- stronger crash/retry, checkpoint and run-provenance contracts;
- explicit treatment of censored/terminated runs in aggregates;
- structural and numeric sensitivity support across scientific configuration;
- clearer nominal-versus-realized mechanism observability;
- additional deterministic and invariant checks across spatial/demographic integrations;
- fail-closed identifiability and equifinality analysis before calibration or competing-hypothesis claims.

## Demographic-baseline finding

A replicated post-audit demographic study tested the frozen synthetic demographic controls across household structure, founder-age and resource-productivity sensitivity.

The 288-run exploratory factorial was followed by an independent 384-run confirmation using 64 new paired process seeds. The conclusion is deliberately negative but scientifically useful: **AnthroSim should not designate a universal demographic baseline at present.**

In the confirmatory central contrast:

- `replacement_control_v1` with fixed-founder households realized about -0.370% late growth per year and mean year-240 population 50.3;
- the same replacement schedule with deterministic fission realized about -1.743% per year and mean year-240 population 8.2;
- `positive_growth_control_v1` with fixed-founder households was approximately stationary at -0.002% per year (95% MC CI -0.062% to +0.058%) with 11.3% mate limitation;
- the same positive schedule under deterministic fission realized about -0.994% per year with 38.7% mate limitation and mean year-240 population 28.9 rather than 108.2.

The result demonstrates strong model-form dependence of realized demography. `replacement_control_v1` therefore remains an intrinsic replacement control rather than a guarantee of realized stationarity. Future scientific studies must explicitly declare and justify demographic and household assumptions.

The analysis also preserves the distinction between diagnosis and tuning: the observed demographic drag motivates decomposition of mating/eligibility/household/spatial constraints, not adjustment of fertility or mortality merely to force a flat population curve.

## Identifiability and equifinality gate

v0.3.1 adds a TRACE analysis procedure that prevents a calibration from collapsing several equally compatible explanations into an arbitrary single optimum.

The gate reports acceptable parameter regions, practical identifiability, staged multi-pattern recovery, profile/conditional summaries, pairwise interaction surfaces, structural equifinality and held-out discriminating predictions. Calibration targets remain separate from held-out corroboration evidence.

Its frozen synthetic benchmark intentionally makes two parameters non-identifiable from one ratio observable: `(1,1)`, `(2,2)`, `(3,3)` and `(4,4)` fit exactly. Adding an independent sum observable reduces the acceptable region to `(2,2)`. A separate structural benchmark verifies that observationally equivalent model structures fail a mechanism-identification claim while preserving a held-out discriminator.

## Compatibility and model identity

This release increments the software version to `0.3.1`. Release-only metadata preparation does not itself change model semantics. Exact Git source identity remains part of run provenance and should be preserved with every scientific result.

Historical synthetic validation controls and preserved scientific reference experiments remain versioned evidence rather than empirical claims about prehistory.
